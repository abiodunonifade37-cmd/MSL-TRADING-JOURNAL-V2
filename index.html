How to read the display

1. Delta is not direction by itself.

Positive delta with falling price can indicate absorption or a mismatch; context decides.

2. CVD divergence needs a defined reference swing.

Compare price highs/lows with cumulative delta at comparable pivots, not arbitrary points.

3. Confirm location first.

A flow signal in the middle of a range is weaker than one at a planned liquidity/HTF area.
